# 2026 agentic video editing landscape notes

FlickSmith was intentionally designed after comparing contemporary agent-native editors and current research rather than assuming MCP support alone is novel.

Current projects already demonstrate multi-track timelines, MCP control, FFmpeg execution, semantic search, version history, OTIO interchange, motion graphics, and post-render verification. Those capabilities are treated as baseline.

Research directions incorporated into FlickSmith include:

- active evidence seeking for long video understanding rather than exhaustive indiscriminate captioning
- multimodal narrative understanding rather than transcript-only selection
- executable/verifiable planning before final timeline creation
- iterative inspect/revise loops for long-horizon creative work
- realistic raw-footage-to-final-cut evaluation rather than isolated edit operations

FlickSmith's response is architectural: Coverage Graph and coverage debt, enforceable Semantic Locks, Intent Receipts, inspectable Edit Memory, Video Git semantic diffs, renderer independence, rights provenance, and localized Repair Lanes.
